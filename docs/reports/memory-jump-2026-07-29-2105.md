## Why memory jumped at 21:00 ET

1. Samsung's headline profit was not truly new: preliminary guidance on July 7
   already called for roughly KRW 171tn sales and KRW 89.4tn operating profit.
   The full release and 10:00 KST conference-call window confirmed the record
   quarter and supplied the detail the market was waiting for.
2. The confirmed numbers are fundamentally strong: operating profit around
   KRW 89.49tn, roughly 19 times the prior-year figure, with AI/HBM and broader
   DRAM/NAND pricing providing the engine.
3. The sector was extremely oversold after forced deleveraging and liquidation.
   Strong results removed an immediate fundamental fear and triggered short
   covering.
4. Confirmation appeared simultaneously in multiple instruments:
   SK Hynix reclaimed 1.35m, MU reclaimed 735, SKHY reclaimed 126 and EWY
   reclaimed 146 between 21:00 and 21:04 ET.
