WHAT EACH NUMBER IS
need% = how far the underlying must move to reach break-even.
EM% = the expiry expected move, measured from the ATM straddle.
n/EM = need divided by EM. The ranking metric.
toll = (ask-bid)/(|delta|*spot), the gate amendment for high-delta contracts.

VETOES ALREADY APPLIED
Expiry containing earnings, strike on the far side of the wall, spread above the 5% gate,
premium over $200, OI under 500, volume under 100, delta outside 0.15-0.62, mid under $0.20.

FRESHNESS
CBOE delayed chain, captured premarket: it reflects the 12 August close. Good for PICKING a
contract, not for firing one. Re-check spread and print live before paying, a closing spread
can open twice as wide. A delayed level never triggers an order.

Model probabilities are lognormal Black-Scholes with each contract own IV. MODEL numbers, not
measured hit rates. Signal only. Not financial advice.
